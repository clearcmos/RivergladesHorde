# Changelog

## v0.3.6

- Hunting loops lead through the mobs instead of around the edge of their area. Each waypoint now counts as reached within 25 yards instead of 40, and no gap between two waypoints is longer than 300 yards. The loops come from QuestieDB's latest spawn data.
- Chapter 3 picks up It Takes Guts first: ride past the port to the Painted Hide, then Argo gives the quest during the port pickups. Every beast drops Beast Entrails, so Trapclaw and the coast crawlers count toward the 15, and the entrails loop near the port only finishes what is left.
- G45-B4G, the six Bloodmaster's Ring opponents and the Rog'mar Trainees get RXP's target button.
- Steps in chapters 1, 2 and 3 moved: if you are in one of them, click your current step in RXP's step list.

## v0.3.5

- Old Rot-Chum, Trapclaw and Scoutmaster Vargas get one marker at their spot instead of a loop around the area. Old Rot-Chum's marker is Wowhead's spot for him (77.4, 72.2). Steps in chapter 3 shifted: if you are in chapter 3, click your current step in RXP's step list.

## v0.3.4

- An optional auction house step at the start of chapter 1 lists the items you can buy ahead for Step Two: More Rock (10 Heavy Weightstones) and Not My Circus (10 Briny Seafood Stew). RXP hides it in solo self-found mode. Every later step of chapter 1 moved by one: if you are in chapter 1, click your current step in RXP's step list.
- Step Two: More Rock, Not My Circus and Hail the Dragonslayer show their turn-in only once you have the items, instead of as soon as you take the quest.

## v0.3.3

- Fuzzle's arrow points to where he stands in Sunnyglade (47.4, 51.4).
- The Farholde Scouting Report reminder shows only while you fight Farholde's soldiers, not across the whole first chapter. Steps 46 to 56 of chapter 1 moved: if you were on one of them, click your current step in RXP's step list.

## v0.3.2

- Bristle Hills Bandits is offered at the second Kreza visit only; she does not have it at the first.
- Show Them How It's Done is offered only after Food for the Taking, which is when Krom'rosh gives it, so the early "skip this if not offered" step is gone.
- Renamed to Riverglades Horde, since CurseForge does not allow a release stage in a project name. It is still experimental. RXP lists the guide under the new name, so pick it again once, then click your current step in RXP's step list.
- Quest items you use (Wood Bundle, Makeshift Stickin' Spear, Remote Explosive Charge) get RXP's clickable item button on their steps.
- If a Farholde Scouting Report drops, a step offers to start it and the next Krom'rosh visit turns it in.
- Each hunting area is one step that lists every objective there with live counters, instead of a "while you're here" step plus a "finish" step for the same item. Objectives for quests you don't have are skipped.

## v0.3.1

- Initial release. If you installed an earlier copy by hand, delete its `CMOS_Riverglades` folder.
